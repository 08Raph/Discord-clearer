Discord Message Cleaner deletes YOUR OWN Discord messages from DMs, group DMs and servers.

Please read before installing:

- Deleted messages cannot be recovered. Always run a Preview first.
- Using automation on a user account goes against Discord's Terms of Service.
  Your account could be limited or banned. You use this app at your own risk.
- Your token is only sent to discord.com. It is never saved unless you tick
  "Remember me", and then it's encrypted with your Windows account.
- This app is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Discord Inc.
